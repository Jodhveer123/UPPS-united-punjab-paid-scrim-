UPPS Tournament Rules & Guidelines

All players must read these guidelines before registering. Tournament-specific announcements and instructions published by the UPPS organizers also apply. If a tournament's event page states a specific format, fee, start time or scoring method, read it carefully before joining.

1. Player eligibility and accounts

  • Provide accurate account details and the correct Free Fire UID and in-game name.
  • Use your own account and do not impersonate another player or share misleading registration information.
  • For duo and squad events, register the correct teammates and follow the team-size instructions shown on the event page.
  • Contact support if you notice a mistake in your registration before the match begins.

2. Tournament formats and event details

  • Events may use Solo, Duo or Squad formats. Check the specific tournament page for its format, capacity, entry fee, rules, start time and scoring method.
  • Do not assume every tournament uses the same scoring system. Follow the scoring and placement rules published for that event.
  • Only registrations confirmed by the platform or organizer should be treated as accepted.

3. Registration and paid entry

  • Read the event details before paying or submitting a registration.
  • For paid events, follow the payment instructions provided by UPPS and submit only genuine payment information or proof when requested.
  • A payment submission is not necessarily a confirmed registration; wait for the status or confirmation from the organizer.
  • Do not send payment to unofficial accounts or people claiming to represent UPPS.

4. Refunds and customer support

  • Players may request a refund by contacting UPPS customer support.
  • Support will review the request and communicate the outcome and any next steps.
  • Include your registered username, tournament name and relevant payment or registration reference when contacting support. Never share your password or one-time verification codes.
  • A refund is not automatic merely because a request is submitted. Contact support for review; do not rely on unofficial promises or messages.

Refund requests are handled through customer support. These guidelines do not promise a fixed processing time or automatic approval.

5. Match readiness and punctuality

  • Be online and ready at least 5 minutes before the scheduled match time.
  • Allow time to read organizer messages and resolve access issues before the match starts.
  • If you expect a problem, contact support or the organizer as early as possible. Late arrival may affect your ability to participate.

6. Room ID, password and announcements

  • Room ID and password will be posted on the UPPS website when the organizer releases them for that event.
  • Check the tournament page and your player dashboard regularly for room details, schedule changes, match instructions and results.
  • Do not share room credentials publicly or with people who are not registered for that match.
  • Only trust updates published by UPPS through its official website or official community channels.

7. Fair play and prohibited conduct

  • Cheats, hacks, unauthorized tools, exploits used to gain an unfair advantage, impersonation and deliberate match manipulation are prohibited.
  • In Solo events, teaming with other players is prohibited unless the event rules explicitly say otherwise.
  • Do not harass, threaten, abuse or discriminate against other players, staff or organizers.
  • Do not falsify results, payment proof, screenshots or registration details.

8. Match issues, results and disputes

  • Follow the instructions announced by the organizer for the specific match.
  • If there is a technical issue, suspected cheating or result discrepancy, contact support promptly and provide truthful details and relevant evidence.
  • Results and prize information will be published or communicated by the organizer through the available UPPS channels.
  • Do not assume a prize is confirmed until the official result and prize details have been checked.

9. Violations and organizer decisions

  • Violations may lead to a warning, removal from a match, disqualification or registration review depending on the circumstances and event rules.
  • Organizers may investigate reports and ask for relevant evidence before making a decision.
  • Contact customer support respectfully if you want a decision reviewed.

10. Keep checking UPPS

Players are responsible for checking the website regularly before their event. Important information—including room details, schedule changes, organizer instructions and results—may be published there. Save the tournament page and dashboard for quick access.
